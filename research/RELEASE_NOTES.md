# Research collection v0.1

Original source archives and selected papers supporting the Zebra Finch Project research catalog.

- zebra-finch-atlas.zip: Poirier and colleagues, 3D MRI atlas; https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22107510; CC BY 4.0. Related article: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2008.01.069.
- ML4BL_ZF.zip: Zandberg, Morfi, George, Clayton, Stowell and Lachlan; https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5545872; CC BY 4.0. Contains 887 real syllable WAV files and perceptual data.
- kv3-data.xlsx and kv3-README.md: original files from https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8187898; CC0. Creator and citation metadata are preserved in research/evidence/zenodo-8187898.json.
- elife-01833.pdf: Hamaguchi et al. (2014), https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.01833; CC BY.
- elife-07770.pdf: Gill et al. (2015), https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.07770; CC BY.

All files are unchanged originals, although release filenames are simplified for the atlas and Kv3 files. The acquisition manifest includes original URLs and SHA-256 hashes; Zenodo files additionally passed provider checksum verification. Licenses remain with their original authors. CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ ; CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .

The MRI coordinate-scale discrepancy is unresolved; consult research/QUALITY.md before anatomical measurements. This is a research starter collection, not a validated atlas conversion or reproduced model. Original movies are committed under media/original. BirdPark annotations and the exploratory EM API sample were acquired locally; they are not mirrored in this release. Their providers remain linked in the catalog.
