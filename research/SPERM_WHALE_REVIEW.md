# Sperm whale collection — 9 November 2026

Seven primary article records were added using Europe PMC core metadata and indexed abstracts, saved in evidence/marine. Only Sharma et al. (2024), DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-47221-8, and the Svalbard monitoring study, DOI 10.1038/s41598-024-56287-9, received publisher full-text and data-availability inspection in this pass. The other five records are leads with that narrower review status displayed in the library.

Sharma Figure 1 is the unchanged original publisher rendition, licensed CC BY 4.0. It is a published explanatory schematic and exchange plot, not a diagram generated by this project. The paper-linked DominicaCodas.csv was parsed with Python CSV and NumPy. Its SHA-256 and derived count, median and quantile tables are in marine-coda-summary.json. No publisher notebook or pickle was executed; clustering and inferential results have not been replicated. No standalone repository license was located, so the CSV remains local and the site links to its provider.

The deposited file contains 8,719 rows: 7,770 EC1 and 949 EC2. The article describes 8,719 EC1 codas. The reason for that difference is unresolved. Retain all supplied labels, including NOISE categories, and distinguish a deposited-file summary from the published analysis cohort. This is an acquisition/cohort reconciliation issue, not evidence of misconduct. Codas are repeated within animals and encounters; do not infer animal-level significance from row counts.

Five unmodified supplementary WAV files were extracted from the Svalbard paper's publisher ZIP, checked for finite samples with SciPy, and inventoried with hashes. These are buzz, slow-click and usual-click recordings, not the Caribbean social coda dataset. Article CC BY 4.0 applies; preserve article and supplementary figure references. The mother/calf photograph is Gabriel Barathieu's Mauritius photograph under CC BY-SA 2.0, and does not depict identified study subjects.

No sperm whale MRI or neural circuit dataset was acquired. Acoustic pattern structure is not a measurement of neural connectivity or proof of semantic meaning. The marine collection does not fabricate an atlas, tractogram or simulation.
